- **A Visualizations page.** A new page under Reference shows every chart the suite draws, each with example data you can click through, plus a searchable catalogue of more than two hundred analysis figures, each linked to the analysis that produces it.
- **More figures for more tools.** Spelling suggestions, repeated sentences, extractive summaries, annotated-word search, NRC emotions, keyness, readability, name gender, coreference, dispersion, n-grams, duplicate documents and knowledge graphs now draw their own figures.
- **Maps in the app.** Geocoding, GIS and subject-verb-object maps draw on a built-in world basemap, and a coverage chart shows how many place names were found. The offline geocoder knows a limited set of places; choose an online provider for wider coverage.
- **New chart types on the canvas.** Pie, sunburst, treemap, violin, radar, waffle, calendar, Sankey and cumulative-distribution charts are drawn directly in the app.
- **Fixes in figures.** The emotions-over-time figure no longer fails on dated corpora (an undated one now asks you to add dates or an order, as other figures over time do), and the document-similarity network reads every pair instead of only near-duplicates.
- **Many smaller fixes** across import (CSV, HTML, UTF-16 text), Compare, MALLET topic models, maps and Google Earth tours, Scripts, backups and the model checks in Settings.
- **Updating:** install 0.5.2 over your current version; your projects are kept. One-click in-app updates are not switched on yet.
